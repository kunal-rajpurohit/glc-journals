✎ How to change anything on the website

This guide is for everyone. You do not need to know any computer code. If you can type in a letter, you can do all of this.

The golden rule: only change the words. Leave the boxes, buttons and pictures where they are. If you make a mistake, press Ctrl + Z to undo, or just close the page without pressing Save.

1. Sign in

1
Open the website. Click Log in at the very top right.
2
Type your email and password. Click Log in.
Use the email kunalrajpurohit@glcmumbai.example and the password you were given separately. Janeway always asks for an email address, not a plain name, so type the full address above. Change the password straight away (click your name → My profile).
3
You will now see a pale yellow bar at the top of the page. That means you can edit.

2. Change words on any page

1
Go to the page you want to change.
2
Look for the yellow ✎ Edit button. On the home page there is one on every section. On other pages it sits at the bottom right of the screen.
3
Click it. A page opens with a big writing box. Click inside the box and type, just like in a letter.
4
Click the green Save button at the bottom.
5
Go back to the website and press F5. Your new words are there.

3. Change the menu at the top

1
Click Change the menu in the yellow bar.
2
You see a list of menu names. Click a name to change it. Click Save.
3
To add a new menu button, use the form on the same page: type the Display name (what people see) and the Link (where it goes), then save.

4. Change the colours of a journal

1
Click All pages in the yellow bar. Click the page called Site Colours.
2
You will see two colour codes that start with #. The first is the main colour. The second is the accent colour. Change them to any colour code you like.
3
Click Save. The whole journal changes colour.
Keep the main colour dark, so the white words on top of it are easy to read. Good colour codes can be found by searching “colour picker” on the internet.

5. Change a picture

All pictures right now say TEST IMAGE. Replace them with real photos like this:

1
Open the folder src/static/GLCLaw/img/ on the computer that runs the website. Open the folder of your journal (for example criminal).
2
Put your photo in that folder. Name it something simple, for example my-moot.jpg.
3
Click the yellow ✎ Edit button on the section with the old picture. Click the picture, then click the picture icon in the toolbar and change the file name from photo-1.svg to my-moot.jpg. Click Save.
Not sure? The easy way: give your new photo the same name as the old one (for example photo-1.svg becomes photo-1.jpg) and ask your helper to point the page to it once. After that you never touch it again.

6. Add a new page

1
Click All pages in the yellow bar, then Add New Page.
2
Link: one word with dashes, like our-sponsors. Display name: the title people see, like Our Sponsors.
3
Type your words in the big box. Click Save. The page is at /your-journal/site/our-sponsors/.
4
To put it in the menu, follow section 3 and use that same address as the Link.

7. Publish an issue or an article

The home page fills itself in. The newest issue, its articles, the news list and "From the Archive" all come from what you publish. You never type them on the home page.
1
Click your name at the top right, then Manager.
2
Open Articles & Issues to create a new issue. Open Submission and the review boxes to follow papers sent in by authors. Janeway guides you step by step on each screen.
3
Once an issue is published, it shows up on the Current Issue & Archives page by itself.
